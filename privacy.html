Loupio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Loupio is an antique identifier: it estimates what a piece is and gives an honest price range, reads maker's marks and keeps your collection in a cabinet on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your cabinet — lots, estimates, notes, shelves, sale prices and every photo you save — is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server copy of your cabinet.

Photos you choose to examine

When you examine a piece, and only after you agree in the app, the photo of the piece (and of its mark, if you add one) is sent through our own server to OpenAI's vision service to produce the estimate. It is used only to answer that request. Our server does not store the photos. OpenAI processes them under its API data policy (API inputs are not used to train their models). You can withdraw consent any time in Settings.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription and to count paywall views and purchases; it receives an anonymous app profile ID, your quiz answers (what you collect, where from, why) and purchase events — not your name or your cabinet. We don't use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and we don't collect IDFA.

Estimates

Estimates are for reference only and are not a certified appraisal.

Children

Loupio is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting data

Remove any lot in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
