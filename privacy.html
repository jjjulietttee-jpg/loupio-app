Privacy: what leaves the cabinet

Short version: your pieces live on your phone, and a photo travels only when you ask for an estimate.

Lot 2The one trip a photo makes

When you ask for an estimate, Loupio shrinks the picture (longest side 1,024 pixels), turns it into a JPEG and posts it over an encrypted connection to a cloud function we run on Google Cloud (region us-central1). That function relays it, with a fixed instruction prompt, to an AI model hosted through Replicate: Google Gemini first, and an OpenAI model as a standby if the first does not answer in time. The reply comes back as a short slip: kind of object, era, origin, material, maker, range and reasons.

Our function keeps the images in memory only while the request runs. We do not write them to a database or storage bucket and we do not build a library of user photos. The function notes request failures in operational logs and applies a rate limit per network address over short windows; that limit is why your address passes through it. The model hosts apply their own handling and retention rules to what they process, and we have no control over those rules.

Lot 3Consent comes first

Before the first photo leaves, Loupio asks whether you agree to send pictures to the appraisal service. You can change your mind at any time in Settings, Photos and AI, Withdraw consent. After that the app asks again before the next estimate, and without a yes, nothing is sent.

Lot 4What stays in the cabinet

Each piece you keep is saved as a JPEG (longest side 1,600 pixels) in the app's private storage on the phone, while titles, categories, ranges and notes sit in the app's on-device records. Camera access is used to take the shots; if you choose an existing picture instead, iOS hands Loupio only that picture. Loupio does not read the rest of your library.

Our own servers have no account for you. We do not ask for a name or an e-mail address, and nothing in the cabinet is synced to us.

Lot 5Taking things back

  • Open a piece and tap Remove from cabinet: the entry and its stored photo are erased from the phone.
  • Delete the app and everything it saved goes with it.
  • Photos already processed by the appraisal service cannot be pulled back by us, because we do not hold them; see lot 2.

Lot 6Subscription and diagnostics

Purchases are handled by Apple. Loupio uses Adapty to read whether Pro is active and to show the right offer; Adapty works with an anonymous profile number and purchase events, not with your pictures. If you use Write to us inside the app, your mail app opens with the app version, iOS version and that profile number pre-filled so we can find your purchase. Delete those lines if you prefer.
