Terms of use

The ground rules for Loupio, in the order you are likely to need them.

Clause 1Pro is how you use the app

Appraising a piece needs an active Pro subscription; Loupio has no free examinations. Plans are a weekly one (new subscribers who qualify get a three-day free trial) and a yearly one. The exact price in your currency, and whether a trial is on offer to you, is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm.

Clause 2Billing and renewal

Payment is taken by Apple through your Apple ID once you confirm. Unless you turn auto-renew off a day or more ahead of the closing date of the running term, the plan rolls over into another term of equal length. When you buy, whatever is left of a free trial is forfeited. To stop renewal, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Loupio (the app's Manage or cancel subscription button goes there too). Deleting the app does not cancel anything. Refunds are decided by Apple.

Clause 3An estimate is not an appraisal

Ranges are produced by an AI model from your photo and are for general reference. Condition, provenance, restoration and market mood can move real value far outside a range. Loupio is not a certified appraiser and nothing it shows may be relied on for insurance, sale, tax, inheritance or legal purposes. Where the app flags a piece for a specialist, take that seriously.

Clause 4Your photos and your conduct

Photograph only things you may photograph, and do not upload anything unlawful or about other people's private lives. Do not try to copy, resell, reverse engineer or overload the appraisal service, or pass around its output as a commercial data feed.
