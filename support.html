DeskCommon questions

I paid, but Loupio still shows the paywall.

Open Settings inside Loupio and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro. If it still fails, mail us the Apple order number.

How do I cancel?

On the iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Loupio, Cancel. The in-app Manage or cancel subscription button opens that same screen.

How do I get a piece out of my cabinet?

Open the piece and tap Remove from cabinet. The entry and its photo are gone from the phone right away.

Is my photo kept after an estimate?

Not by us. It passes through our relay to the AI model and the answer returns; see Privacy, lot 2, for the providers involved.

Loupio keeps asking before sending a photo.

You withdrew consent, or you have not given it yet. Agree on the prompt, or check Settings, Photos and AI.

The estimate says "ask an appraiser".

That marker appears for pieces that could be worth over about $1,000, for ones that might be an original or a reproduction, and when the model is unsure. It is a sign to get a human opinion, not an error.

The estimate failed or timed out.

Check your connection and try a clearer, well-lit photo. A failed attempt should not use up your short-term allowance. If the service is busy, wait a minute and retry.
