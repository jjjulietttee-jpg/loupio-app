The Loupio Cabinet

Pieces you photograph, ranges you can reason about, and the paperwork that goes with them.

EntryWhat this site is

Loupio helps you look at an object you inherited, bought second-hand or found in a shed. You photograph it, optionally add a shot of its maker's mark, and the app answers with a probable identification and a price range in US dollars, plus the reasons behind that range. This site holds the three documents that govern the app.

Read firstThe honest limits

An estimate here is an informed guess from a photograph. It is never a certified appraisal, an insurance valuation or a promise that anyone will pay the stated range. For anything that looks valuable, the app itself tells you to consult a qualified human appraiser.
